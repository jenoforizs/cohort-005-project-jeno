# Cadence Glossary

The canonical language for Cadence's domain. Code, UI copy and emails all use these terms; when a term below lists words to _Avoid_, those words appear nowhere in the product.

## Refunds

**Refund request**:
A learner's request to get their money back for one purchase. Every refund request has exactly one status: Open, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, "Cancel my purchase"

**Refund**:
The money sent back to the learner when a refund request is Approved. A refund request that is Open or Declined has no refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Open**:
The status of a refund request the team has not yet resolved. A purchase has at most one open refund request at a time.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested

**Approved**:
The status of a refund request the team accepted: a refund is sent and the learner's access to the course ends.

**Declined**:
The status of a refund request the team rejected: no refund is sent.

**Refund amount**:
What the learner paid for the purchase. An approved refund request always sends back the refund amount, whatever the course's list price is now.

**Refund window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase, including all of the 14th day, during which a learner can raise a refund request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refundable purchase**:
A purchase that can get a refund request: it has no open refund request, is inside its refund window, has under 2 hours of video watched, has under half its lessons completed, and is not a claimed team seat.

**Chargeback**:
A card dispute the cardholder raises through their bank against a payment. Cadence does not model chargebacks, so this word never names a refund request or a refund.
